## Concept: "The Company File"

Home argues, Services schedules, About introduces: who Ares is, what it promises, who
stands the post, and the one document that sums it up.

1. **Opening:** the Pikes Peak photograph full bleed (a real place, so it carries a mono
   caption), headline low-left. Under it, the four certification marks in a row, each
   with its identifier in mono, sliding in one after another.
2. **01 Company:** "Quality over quantity." One paragraph, a facts ledger (founded,
   ownership, licenses, service area), and the vehicles photograph as a captioned plate.
3. **02 Commitments:** one intro line, then four commitments to clients and four to our
   people. Desktop: two ruled lists side by side. Phones: a two-tab switch, so only four
   short rows show at a time.
4. **03 People:** "Who stands your post." The officer portrait and the five traits set
   large, each with one line.
5. **04 Statement:** page 1 of the real capability statement as a plate that settles flat
   as it scrolls in, beside "Everything above, on one page." with View, Download, quote,
   and phone.

Cut: the "Raising the bar" grey band, the eight-tile values grid (folded into the two
lists), the glass capability card, bracketed labels, the breadcrumb, and duplicated
claims. The page goes from about 1,100 words to about 450.
