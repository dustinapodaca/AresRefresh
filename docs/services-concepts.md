# Services: two concepts (2026-10-05)

Both keep the Dossier system fixed (DESIGN.md) and all six divisions with their Matrix
photos. Content source: `docs/services-content.md`.

## Concept A: "The Schedule" (picked)

Read like the services section of a contract file: a contents page, then numbered
entries, then the method, then where and how to start.

1. **Opening (cover page):** the headline and lead on the left; on the right, an index of
   the six divisions (mono 01 to 06 on hairline rows) that jumps to each entry. Quote
   button and phone under the lead. No photo, so the first screen reads as a contents
   page rather than a second Home hero.
2. **01 Divisions:** six entries on hairline-strong rules. Each entry: the Matrix photo
   as a plate (five columns, mono caption), and beside it the number, title, body, and a
   two-column "Covers" list (the old pills). Government carries a GSA eLibrary link, so
   the one proof claim on the page can be checked.
3. **02 Process (the pinned moment):** on desktop the headline and the "why" paragraph
   hold still on the left while the four stages scroll past on the right, on a vertical
   hairline track that fills as you read. The stage crossing the middle reads at full ink.
   On mobile it is the same track, unpinned.
4. **03 Areas:** the three city rows, as on Home (links to the location pages), ending
   on a hand-off line.
5. **Cross-boundary photograph:** the Denver skyline (`about-banner.jpg`, the one
   Colorado place in the Services set) runs full bleed under the end of 03 Areas and
   ends under the opening of 04 Contact.
6. **04 Contact:** the same close as Home (headline, phone, pairs, button) over the rust
   glow. The footer no longer docks a card for /services.
7. **Rail:** 01 Divisions, 02 Process, 03 Areas, 04 Contact; running head on mobile.

Rhythm: open cover, dense entries, held passage, tight list, full-bleed image, open close.

## Concept B: "The Exhibit Wall"

1. **Opening:** the Denver skyline full bleed behind the headline, as Home does.
2. **Divisions as a pinned passage:** the photo holds still on the left and swaps as each
   division scrolls past on the right (a single sticky frame cycling six photos).
3. **Process:** a horizontal four-column track with phase labels under it.
4. **Close:** the Home contact close.

## Why A

B's sticky frame would show the 640px Matrix photos at half the page width, where they
go soft on a retina screen, and swapping photos needs a fade that starts from zero,
which DESIGN.md rules out. B's photo hero also repeats Home's first screen, so the two
pages would blur together. A keeps each photo next to the words it illustrates, at a
size the files can carry, and its index-first opening gives Services its own entrance
while staying in the same document. A horizontal four-column process (B) also squeezes
60-word stage bodies into 250px columns; A's vertical track reads at full measure and
gives the page its one pinned moment.
