# Inspiration board (Mobbin, 2026-10-04)

Patterns, not pixels. Each entry names the one idea taken, where it lands on the Ares
Home, and how it changes to fit a federal security contractor. Six entries are the
owner's starting references (marked **owner ref**); the rest come from one-pattern
searches.

Searches run: editorial long-form dark homepages; transitions between dark sections
without dividers; large pull-quote testimonials; careers sections on dark editorial
sites; closing contact CTAs on long pages; security, defense, and government-contractor
homepages; spec-sheet and documentation-style data; pinned scroll sections.

Rejected on sight: tile grids of stats (Superpower results, Eat Real Food bars), boxed
team cards (Origin), carousel testimonials (Shopify), framed product panels (Fauna,
Giga voice), glassy nav capsules, gradient art (Windsurf).

### 1. Atlas: the photograph as a plate (owner ref)
- Link: https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2f379c3d-dfcb-4eb2-8a2f-d01cc298f5f5
- Pattern: one photograph set inset on black, with a quiet index of related frames under
  it. The photo is presented like evidence, not decoration.
- Ares section: 02 Staffing.
- Adaptation: the officer portrait sits as a single plate with a mono caption line
  underneath (what the photo shows). No thumbnail strip, and no vertical frame rules
  (those belonged to a rejected pass).

### 2. Giga: a dark field lit from the horizon (owner ref)
- Link: https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f850ffc1-be4a-4064-88d5-9b52d2ec9d52
- Pattern: near-black hero with a single low warm light rising from the bottom edge, and
  type left-aligned in the lower half.
- Ares section: 05 Contact (the page's close).
- Adaptation: the owner's rust (sampled from the copper glass in the hero photo) becomes
  a low horizon glow behind the closing phone number. Light only, never a fill, and it
  appears once.

### 3. Garden: opposite corners and an empty middle (owner ref)
- Link: https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/38333d73-f428-421e-bfe3-c954e3926136
- Pattern: a short label pinned top-left, a paragraph pinned top-right, and a large
  quiet field between them. Restraint does the work.
- Ares section: 01 Verify opening.
- Adaptation: the Verify headline sits left and a two-line instruction ("check every
  number against SAM.gov and GSA eLibrary") sits right on the same baseline, with open
  space before the ledger starts. No eyebrow label; the rail mark does that job.

### 4. Superpower: a numbered index that tracks the story (owner ref)

## Round 2: breaking up mobile (2026-10-04)

The owner flagged that mobile read as one long column of text. These references show
how other sites pace a long page on a phone.
