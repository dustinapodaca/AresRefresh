# Services inspiration board (Mobbin, 2026-10-05)

Patterns, not pixels. Each entry names the one idea taken, where it lands on Services,
and how it changes to fit the Dossier system (DESIGN.md). The Home board
(`docs/inspiration.md`) still applies for the rail, ledger, close, and bleed photograph.

Searches run: numbered service indexes on dark pages; service detail rows with a photo
and a spec list; four-phase process timelines; sticky image beside a scrolling service
list; services page headers with a table of contents.

Rejected on sight: tile grids of service cards (Attio, Mailchimp, Raw Materials boxed
columns), card carousels with active fills (Overmind), icon timelines (Apollo, Duolingo),
image-swap galleries (MindMarket hover cards), team card grids (Origin).

---

### 1. Runway Studios: an index under the opening
- Link: https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/754769a3-efe7-48cb-98bb-547e66bfdfee
- Pattern: a plain index of everything on the page, set as a short list beside the intro.
- Services section: the page opening.
- Adaptation: the six divisions sit as a numbered index (mono 01 to 06 on hairline rows)
  beside the headline. Each row jumps to its division. It is the table of contents of
  the file.

### 2. Koto: the jump-to row and the label column
- Links: https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2ce48fe2-bea1-438e-a2aa-0b1af12f7996 (header),
  https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/968754c4-c525-4315-a823-07721be58b1b (service block)
- Pattern: a service name in a narrow left column, one sentence beside it, then its
  sub-items as a ruled list underneath.
- Services section: each division entry.
- Adaptation: the division title and body sit beside the photo; the site types (the old
  pills) become a two-column ruled list labeled "Covers". No tags, no pills.

### 3. Locomotive: a photo beside a category list
- Link: https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/0789240f-37f9-4452-b1f9-2e29b8a7ff6e
- Pattern: one modest photograph on the left, the category and its list on the right.
- Services section: division entries.
- Adaptation: the Matrix photo is set as a plate (4px corners, hairline edge, mono
  caption) at five columns, which is about as large as the 640px source files hold up.

### 4. basement.studio: headline, plate, paragraph
- Link: https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/caa1a6b1-4b01-4b6a-8d30-df5454015bbf
- Pattern: a big headline, a restrained photo, and a paragraph, each in its own column.
- Services section: the opening's proportions (headline left, index right).
- Adaptation: no photo in the opening; the index takes the photo's column so the first
  screen reads as a cover page, not a second Home hero.

### 5. Dovetail: a vertical stepper with one active step
- Link: https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/0c8a622e-7ba8-4695-8e33-c1a6138dda78
- Pattern: steps on a thin vertical line with square markers; the current step reads
  brighter and the line shows progress.
- Services section: 02 Process.
- Adaptation: four stages on a hairline track. The track fills as the reader scrolls (a
  position indicator) and the stage crossing the middle of the screen reads at full ink.
  All text is always visible; nothing collapses.

### 6. Wild: phase labels in mono
- Link: https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1bac0acb-c4fe-46ec-acd7-fe0bf057faf2
- Pattern: each phase carries a small mono label of what it covers above its name.
- Services section: 02 Process.
- Adaptation: "Scope · Risk · Site walk" and the other micro lines become mono labels
  above each stage name.

### 7. Grok: one warm marker on a cold line
- Link: https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/5809b85f-2e44-4d26-998f-9fb4e2379f13
- Pattern: a hairline timeline in grey with a single warm square for the current point.
- Services section: 02 Process markers.
- Adaptation: the stage markers are small squares on the track; the rail's rust tick
  stays the only rust on the page apart from focus rings and the close's glow.

### 8. Analogue: lists on rules, not boxes (owner ref from Home)
- Link: https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/7063e124-d7b5-4fe4-9797-b318a5be20d1
- Pattern: capabilities grouped as plain lists with a thin rule.
- Services section: the "Covers" lists.
- Adaptation: two columns of Inter 15px in ink on hairline rows.

### 9. Miro: a table of contents that stays put
- Link: https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/47c3e6ab-e2e1-401e-baa3-79e8954e5919
- Pattern: a sticky contents column beside a long document.
- Services section: the document rail.
- Adaptation: the Home rail, with Services' own marks (01 Divisions, 02 Process,
  03 Areas, 04 Contact) and the same running head on mobile.
