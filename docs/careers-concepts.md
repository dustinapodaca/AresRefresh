# Careers: concept ("The Roster", 2026-10-05)

Home argues, Services schedules, About introduces, the Capability Statement serves, the
quote page takes the request. Careers recruits: it reads like the roster an officer would
join, with the roles first and the reasons beside real Ares people.

## Mobbin board
- Runway and Linear "Open roles": a quiet table on hairlines, role, team, location, Apply.
- Runway careers hero: a small rating badge by the headline ("G2 4.8/5"); here, Indeed.
- Faire and Customer.io: the team photograph carries the page.

## The page
- **Opening:** the Colorado Springs skyline (captioned) as a band, "Join the team." and the
  lead low on it, then the Indeed rating as a small proof with its link, "See open roles",
  and the careers email.
- **01 Roles:** the three roles as a table: role and line, type (mono), location, and an
  Apply route that opens an email with the role in the subject.
- **02 Why people stay:** the team at the range beside the four reasons on hairlines, with
  the officers' Indeed rating as its proof.
- **03 How we work:** an Ares officer at the range as a wide plate in the column (the
  photo is 1184px, too small for full bleed), settling as it enters; the well-being
  paragraph under it.
- **04 Apply (the close):** "Send us your résumé." with the email large in mono over the
  rust light.
- **Rail:** its own form, `list`: the section names in Inter, dimmed, the current one in
  ink with a small dot that moves to it (Intercom's list on Mobbin).
