## Concept: "The Statement, Served"

The page is the PDF, made browsable. It must stand out like Home: the strongest first
screen on the site after Home's.

1. **Hero:** the owner-supplied glass-panels light (`hero-panels.webp`) full bleed, black
   from the left. Headline "Capability statement." at display size; the one-line offer;
   the credential line; a white Download (PDF) button plus a quiet "Request a quote".
   Beside it, page 1 of the real statement floats at a slight 3D tilt, catching the
   light, and settles as the page scrolls. Under the copy, a mono spec line (One page ·
   PDF · 2.1MB · 2026 · Ares Security LLC). Below the hero, the four quick facts as
   Home's key row (NAICS, set-aside, UEI with SAM.gov route, CAGE).
2. **01 Capabilities:** six competencies as ruled entries in three columns (number
   beside the title, body, keys as a mono middle-dot line). Phones: rows that fold
   (name and keys visible, body on tap).
3. **02 Why Ares:** the three differentiators in three columns. Phones: a swipe row with
   the Home meter.
4. **03 How to buy:** the GSA MAS as a spec block (Atlas): mono "● Active", four spec rows,
   eLibrary route; beside it the four procurement paths with mono status, and the
   set-aside note.
5. **04 Credentials:** seven marks on hairlines (the four plus Denver and the two
   Colorado Verified), each with its number where one is published; then the full code
   listing as a two-column ledger ("Confirmed against SAM.gov"). Phones: the listing
   folds behind a tappable row.
6. **05 Download (the close):** "Take it with you." with the PDF thumbnail, Download,
   view, quote, phone, over the rust light.
