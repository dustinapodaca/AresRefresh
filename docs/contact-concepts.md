# Request a Quote: concept ("The Intake", 2026-10-05)

A short form page (owner: no section navigator). Home argues, Services schedules, About
introduces, the Capability Statement serves; this page takes the request.

## Mobbin board
- incident.io "Book a demo": dark, labels above filled fields, an image panel that fades
  into the page beside the form.
- Uvodo checkout: the form in numbered parts on hairlines, a summary column beside it.
- Sprig onboarding and Etsy help request: "what do you need" as selectable rows rather
  than a dropdown.
- Superhuman "Talk to sales": the reasons beside a short form.

## The page
- **Opening:** Denver at sunset (`contact-hero.jpg`, a real place, captioned) as a band
  under the nav, masked into the canvas; "Request a quote." and the lead low on it.
- **The intake (left, 6 columns):** three parts on hairlines (unnumbered after the finish
  review: rail marks are the only section numbers). What needs covering (the six
  divisions, the capability statement, other, as radio rows in two columns); About the
  site (the message); How to reach you (name, email, organization, phone). Then the
  discretion line, Send request, an inline status, and the GSA contract as a ledger row.
- **The contact column (right, 5 columns):** "Rather talk?" with the phone in mono and
  the email over the rust light; what happens next (three plain steps from facts on the
  site); the coverage map with the three cities.
- **Phones:** the form first, then the contact column.

Motion: none beyond press, focus, and the radio check. It is a form.
