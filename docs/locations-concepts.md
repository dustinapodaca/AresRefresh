# Location pages: concept

## "The Local File"

Home argues, Services schedules, About introduces, the Capability Statement serves, the
quote page takes intake, Careers recruits. A location page **answers a search and hands
off**. It is one card pulled from the dossier for one city: short, local, and done in
about two screens.

Mobbin (2026-10-06), patterns not copies:
- Airtasker's "near you" landing: the headline answers the search, then a short list of
  local services and one action.
- Upwork's office cards: a small map tile beside the office details.
- Koto and Vizcom: a label left, a ruled typographic list right, each row a link.
- Zendesk's office directory: the other locations listed plainly as onward links.

## The page, top to bottom (Colorado Springs first; Denver and Pueblo reuse it)

1. **Opening.** "Security guards in Colorado Springs." (display-xl, the search phrase),
   a two-sentence lead, and the Front Range drawn to scale beside it: a crop of the
   Colorado map (Denver to Pueblo along I-25) with this city lit in rust and the other two
   faint and linked. A local landmark in mono marks the place (Pikes Peak, COS airport).
   Below 1024px the quote button and call link show under the lead, as on Home.
2. **Licensed here.** Colorado has no statewide license; this city licenses security
   companies itself. The city's license as a ledger: license type and number, training
   provider status, armed endorsements, office. The one proof a local buyer looks for.
3. **What we cover here.** The local sectors as a ruled list in two columns; each row
   links into its Services division.
4. **Where next.** The hand-off: three routes into the main site (Services, Capability
   Statement, About), and the other two cities. No close section, no quote card (the nav
   and the footer carry contact).

No rail (a short page, like the quote page), no photo-led hero (each city page draws its
own map instead; Pueblo has no real photo, and the Springs photos already serve About and
Careers), no section numbers.

## How it differs from siblings

- The only page that opens on a map rather than a photo.
- The only page that ends on routes out rather than a close.
- The ledger shows a city license, which no other page leads with.
